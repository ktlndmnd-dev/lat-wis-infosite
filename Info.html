Women in STEM

The Women in STEM club focuses on the accomplishments and the growth of Women in STEM at our school. Come to our meetings to learn about the other STEM clubs at LAT and join the community! If you are interested in sciences and communications, this club is perfect for you.

We have some awesome stuff planned, like interviewing women who now work in the sciences, fun experiments, and more!

Science Club

Introducing the science club! Where we work together to explore how the world works through exciting, hands-on experiments.

By applying learned scientific concepts to challenges presented within our community and around the world, we can come up with practical solutions that may inspire others to take steps toward positive change. Ready to innovate, investigate, and most importantly, have fun? Join us today!

Youth in Medicine

Introducing the Youth in Medicine club! Where we explore the world of healthcare and medical sciences through engaging discussions, guest speakers, and hands-on activities.

By learning about the latest advancements in medicine and connecting with professionals in the field, we can gain valuable insights into various healthcare careers. Ready to dive into the exciting world of medicine? Join us today!

HOSA

HOSA is Canada's largest student-led organization dedicated to health sciences and healthcare. Come join us to explore different healthcare careers, learn more about medical sciences, and connect with other students who share similar interests! Whether you are thinking about a future in healthcare or simply curious about the human body, everyone is welcome!

We will be participating in conferences, seminars, workshops, and competitions, with plenty of opportunities to develop your leadership and teamwork skills. Come join us and be part of the HOSA community!

Coding and Robotics Club

Join the coding and robotics club to do some fun tech projects, like 3D printing, making websites, and robot races! There is NO experience required to join this club, just come in, and we’ll learn as a group. If you are very interested in robotics, ask either exec about our robotics team and how you can join it!

Be sure to catch our first meeting on November 6th to design and print your own figure or keychain! You do not need to have any 3D modelling experience to participate.
