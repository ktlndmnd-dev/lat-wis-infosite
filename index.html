Welcome!

This is the website for LAT Women in STEM. You can find all of the upcoming and current events happening in the sciences at our school here!

For the 2026-2027 year, we have The Science Club, Youth and Medicine, HOSA, Engineering Club, Coding and Robotics Club, and of course, Women in STEM! You can find information about every club at our school through the website or contacting any of the club heads. Feel free to drop in on any meetings we have going on! It doesn't matter what club it is, if you're interested in it, then we encourage you to join!
